2006 Ford StreetKA 1.6 convertible, as sold in the UK.

Be 80 miles away from home at a car event, shut the boot and as it clicks home, realise you've just locked the only car key you have inside the boot.

The manual lists two ways to open the boot:

1. The usual one is double-press the boot button on the key. Crud.
2. The other one is to open the cubby hole between the two front seats with the key physically, and there's a second keyhole inside which inserting the key and turning unlocks the boot. Also crud.

However, without the key you can also make use of the second method. That lock is underneath the roof cover on the rear of the bulkhead behind the front seats, and the key lock is just attached to a cable which you can pull by hand.

Open the roof cover using the button inside the driver's door jam. (This requires the car to have power and be unlocked.)

Reach inside the roof cavity and in the middle of the car behind the middle of the two seats there's a loop of cable attached to a key lock barrel. Twist it to pull the cable (pull the cable towards the passenger side on a RHD), and hey presto it opens the boot with a manual release.

Remove key from boot, keep it in your pocket all day long.
